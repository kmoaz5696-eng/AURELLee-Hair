About AURELLE

AURELLE is a daily hair oil serum formulated to help restore softness, shine and manageability without weighing hair down.

Product Highlights

Gentle Nourishment

Formulated with Coconut Oil to nourish and hydrate.

Shine & Smoothness

Contains Sunflower Seed Oil for glossy, smooth hair.

Oil-Balance Care

Lightweight, non-greasy feel for balance.

Softness & Comfort

Contains Light Mineral Oil for a soft, comfortable feel.

Aim & Objectives

Aim of the Project

To formulate and develop AURELLE, a daily hair oil serum combining nourishing oils in a suitable cosmetic formulation.

  1. To develop a serum suitable for daily hair care.
  2. To incorporate nourishing oils for hair conditioning.
  3. To include emollients for softness and shine.
  4. To incorporate functional oils such as Coconut and Sunflower Seed Oils.
  5. To obtain a homogeneous formulation with suitable physical characteristics.
  6. To evaluate the final product through quality-control tests.

Why we used these ingredients

Coconut Oil

Chosen for deep conditioning and strength; helps reduce protein loss.

Sunflower Seed Oil

A natural emollient to soften hair and add natural shine.

Tocopherol (Vitamin E)

An antioxidant to help protect hair from damage.

Coconut & Vanilla Fragrance

To give a long-lasting, pleasant scent.

Light Mineral Oil

To give a lightweight, non-greasy feel and smooth spreadability.

Preparation Procedure

  1. 1

    Preparation of Materials

    Prepare and weigh all ingredients accurately according to the approved formulation.

  2. 2

    Preparation of the Oil Phase

    Prepare the oil phase and incorporate the ingredients assigned to this phase.

  3. 3

    Blending of Oils

    Combine Coconut, Sunflower and Light Mineral Oils with gentle mixing.

  4. 4

    Homogenization

    Mix until a homogeneous, clear oil serum is obtained.

  5. 5

    Final Adjustment

    Adjust the formulation according to approved clarity and consistency specs.

  6. 6

    Final Product

    Transfer the finished serum into suitable, clean packaging after QC checks.

Use validated mixing, hygiene and safety procedures throughout preparation.

How to Use

  1. Take a few drops on your palms.
  2. Rub gently.
  3. Apply to dry or damp hair, focusing on the ends.
  4. Style as desired.

Tip

Use a small amount to avoid excess oiliness.

Suitable for all hair types.

Safety & Storage

Precautions

  • For external use only.
  • Avoid contact with eyes.
  • Keep out of reach of children.
  • Discontinue use if irritation occurs.

Storage Conditions

  • Store below 25°C.
  • Keep the bottle tightly closed.
  • Protect from direct sunlight and heat.

Shelf Life

  • Unopened: 24 months.
  • After opening: 12 months.

Possible Side Effects

Rare: mild scalp irritation or allergic reaction.
